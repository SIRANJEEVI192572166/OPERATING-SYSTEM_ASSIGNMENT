Effective access time EAT = (1-p)*100 ns + p*8,000,000 ns
p =      1 in 1,000 (1e-03) : EAT =     8099.9 ns   slow-down =    81.0x
p =    1 in 100,000 (1e-05) : EAT =      180.0 ns   slow-down =     1.8x
p =  1 in 1,000,000 (1e-06) : EAT =      108.0 ns   slow-down =     1.1x

For EAT <= 110 ns: p <= 10/(8,000,000-100) = 1.250e-06  (about 1 fault in 799,990 accesses)
